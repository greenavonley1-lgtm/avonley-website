The Designer

My name is Avonley Vincent, and I am an Apparel Entrepreneurship major with a passion for creativity, innovation, and business.

I chose this major because I have always dreamed of owning my own clothing company and turning my love for fashion into something meaningful and impactful. I have always loved brainstorming new ideas and thinking outside of the box. I especially love exploring ways to make clothing both stylish and functional.

My goal is to build a clothing brand that creates comfortable, wearable pieces for a wide variety of lifestyles and body types. I want my future company to have clothing that not only looks good but truly feels good to wear. I want people to feel comfortable in their own skin while wearing my designs, knowing that the pieces were thoughtfully created with real bodies and real lives in mind. Through my education and hands-on experiences, I am developing the skills needed to become a strong, innovative business owner who leads with creativity, purpose, and passion.
