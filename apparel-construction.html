Apparel Construction

Button Down Shirt Project

In this Apparel Construction project, I created a button-down shirt with a notched collar. I practiced important sewing skills such as cutting fabric, assembling shirt pieces, sewing seams, attaching a collar, adding buttons, and creating a clean finished garment.

This project helped me improve my construction techniques, attention to detail, and understanding of how structured apparel pieces are made.

Linen Wrap Shirt

For this project, I constructed a linen wrap shirt following a 20-step construction flow chart, from pattern preparation through final finishing. The shirt features underlined bodice pieces, a decorative tie panel, gathered puff sleeves finished with lace trim, a faced neckline, and an invisible zipper at center back.

I developed this piece as part of a full line sheet, the Linen Set (Style #1111), and built a complete tech pack covering flat sketches, colorways, fabric and trim sourcing, bill of materials, costing, graded spec measurements, and fit notes.

Patchwork Skirt

For this project, I followed a 13-step construction flow chart to build a patchwork skirt, from cutting and assembling the patchwork panels through attaching the yoke and waistband, inserting an invisible zipper, and finishing the hem.
