Fashion History

Historical Recreation Project

Through my Elizabethan dress recreation project, I gained a deeper appreciation for the history, craftsmanship, and detail involved in Renaissance fashion. Studying traditional silhouettes, fabrics, and construction techniques helped me understand how clothing reflects the culture and values of a specific time period.

Recreating this garment challenged me to apply my apparel construction skills while paying close attention to historical accuracy and design details. This project helped me grow as a designer by teaching me the importance of research, patience, and understanding the story behind a garment before bringing it to life.

Undergarments

As part of the project, I also designed and constructed historically accurate undergarments, including a chemise, pair of bodies, wheel farthingale, and bum roll, which were essential in creating the correct silhouette and structural foundation for the final dress. This project strengthened my skills in historical garment construction, fit, layering, and attention to detail while giving me hands-on experience with complex apparel techniques.
