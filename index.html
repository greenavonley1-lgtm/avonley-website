Apparel Entrepreneurship Portfolio

Avonley Vincent

The purpose of this portfolio is to showcase my academic achievements, coursework, and skill development throughout my program in Apparel Entrepreneurship. This portfolio reflects the knowledge and experience I have gained through my classes, projects, and hands-on learning opportunities. It includes examples of my work in areas such as apparel construction, fashion design, pattern making, fitting and alteration, home decor, social media marketing, and digital design.

Each project highlights my growth as a student and shows how I have developed creative, technical, and professional skills. Through these experiences, I have strengthened my ability to design, construct, problem-solve, communicate, and think critically within the apparel and fashion industry. This portfolio also represents my career goals and my desire to create clothing that is comfortable, inclusive, and helps people feel confident.
