Student Work 07

Pattern Making

Half Scale Project

For this project, I constructed a princess-seamed midi dress at half scale, working from a complete construction guide covering the bodice, sleeves, skirt, and finishing. The dress features a chiffon overlay bodice with a satin underlayer, black piping and cording detail at the princess seams and neckline, a center-front closure with satin rouleau loops and fabric-covered buttons, short puff sleeves with sleeve bands, ivory ribbon bows at the shoulders, and a gathered satin-and-chiffon skirt finished with an invisible center-back zipper.

Download Construction Guide (PDF) ↓

Final Design

For my final design, I developed this concept into a full-scale princess-seamed dress with a scoop neckline finished in black cording, matching piping at the princess seams and basque waist, short chiffon puff sleeves, ivory ribbon bows at the shoulders, and a full gathered satin-and-chiffon skirt. I built a complete construction guide for the piece, covering the bodice, sleeve, and skirt construction through hemming and final finishing.
