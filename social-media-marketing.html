Student Work 08

Social Media Marketing

Picture Post Ad Challenge

In this Social Media Marketing project, I completed a 30-minute challenge where I went to the student store, took a photo of merchandise, and turned it into a postable picture ad. I focused on creating an image that would catch attention, promote the product clearly, and fit the style of a social media post.

This project helped me practice working quickly, using visuals for marketing, and creating content that could be used to advertise a product online.
